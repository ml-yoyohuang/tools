# The Materiality of Motion — pre-implementation direction

The following briefs define inquiry, visual language, composition, palette, typography, choreography, emotion, memorable moment and technical approach before rendering.

## 7 — Nothing keeps its edges

**Inquiry / concept:** Language softens at its boundaries. Separate letters briefly become one continuous body, then settle into a different reading.

MELT is the main image. Warm mineral paper and dark brown ink recall wet letterpress rather than chrome. Opening: a stable broad word. Transformation: individually stretched glyph feet merge through thresholded density. Climax: ME / LT becomes a staggered two-line body. Emotion: sensual but heavy. Local scanline deformation and smooth density union are chosen to make contours, rather than an entire screenshot, change.

**Palette:** #ede6d6 / #40372b / #a74d32.

**Timeline:** 0s I / Legibility → 2s II / Softening → 5s III / Dissolution → 9s IV / Reconstitution → 13s V / Resolution. Duration 16s.

**Planned review positions:** 0, 3.2, 6.4, 9.6, 12.8, 16 seconds.

## 8 — Held, then released

**Inquiry / concept:** A word can store a gesture. A local pull travels through four connected letter bodies, and each region remembers it for a different length of time.

Custom monoline PULL letterforms have generous round internal spaces. Mulberry on dusty rose is punctuated by one pale force marker. Opening: low, wide word at rest. Transformation: a local tensile crest, not whole-letter bouncing. Climax: different stroke sections move out of phase while the word remains continuous. Emotion: poised energy. Cubic path control points follow analytically damped spatial modes.

**Palette:** #dfc4cc / #542c42 / #efe092.

**Timeline:** 0s I / Equilibrium → 2s II / Force → 4s III / Propagation → 6s IV / Oscillation → 10s V / Recovery. Duration 14s.

**Planned review positions:** 0, 2.8, 5.6, 8.4, 11.2, 14 seconds.

## 9 — A letter to the wind

**Inquiry / concept:** An unsent letter has two readings: what the front declares, and what its folds keep out of sight.

An invented unsent letter, with topographic ellipses as wind contours, spans six thin cream pages. Ochre backs carry the concealed sentence. Opening: flat 3×2 poster. Transformation: alternating vertical folds make a small architecture. Climax: horizontal lower leaves turn over while a side leaf reveals its back. Emotion: intimate and intellectual. Actual CSS 3D faces, finite thickness and restrained light-dependent shades preserve text and paper structure.

**Palette:** #a95e43 / #eee8d5 / #dbb25f.

**Timeline:** 0s I / Flatness → 3s II / First fold → 7s III / Expansion → 11s IV / Revelation → 16s V / Unfolding. Duration 20s.

**Planned review positions:** 0, 4, 8, 12, 16, 20 seconds.

## 10 — The angle of a colour

**Inquiry / concept:** Colour is an event between a surface and a viewer. A narrow twisted film makes that event visible without becoming a rainbow ornament.

A single twisted band with a clear open center. Pale neutral surround and mostly silver/olive film; chroma concentrates at oblique edges. Opening: quiet vertical-leaning loop. Transformation: light angle changes before the band flexes. Climax: thin-film wavelength differences expose warm and cool regions. Emotion: contemplative luminosity. A true triangle mesh, computed normals, Fresnel response and three-wavelength interference keep colour tied to geometry. White-material view is a required silhouette test.

**Palette:** #e8e8df / #9aa78f / #b39b77.

**Timeline:** 0s I / Still object → 3s II / Light shift → 6s III / Surface motion → 10s IV / Chromatic climax → 14s V / Quiet resolution. Duration 18s.

**Planned review positions:** 0, 3.6, 7.2, 10.8, 14.4, 18 seconds.

## 11 — Light edits the page

**Inquiry / concept:** A transparent surface becomes an editor: it displaces a word, redraws a boundary and leaves the page intact.

A large word LIGHT, an oxide rectangle and a tightly spaced green rule field supply information for a single glass slab to rearrange. Opening: clean asymmetrical print. Transformation: magnified and displaced letter strokes inside the slab. Climax: the slab bridges the word and the empty field. Emotion: quiet photographic ambiguity. Screen-space refraction uses the same lens-normal field for displacement, tiny spectral separation and edge highlights.

**Palette:** #e8eadf / #284e47 / #b96d42.

**Timeline:** 0s I / Clear composition → 2s II / Interference → 5s III / Refraction → 9s IV / Light composition → 13s V / Departure. Duration 16s.

**Planned review positions:** 0, 3.2, 6.4, 9.6, 12.8, 16 seconds.

## 12 — The shape of an afterimage

**Inquiry / concept:** A single gesture is small. Its past, held beside its present, becomes a room.

A copper arc lives in a charcoal-brown field, offset from an oversized editorial AFTER. Opening: one restrained outline. Transformation: continuous samples form a comb-like volume. Climax: twelve temporal cuts and three translucent exposures make duration visible. Emotion: meditative mystery. Fixed analytic history samples reconstruct every state directly; no frame buffer history or mouse trail is used.

**Palette:** #332f31 / #d49b69 / #e3cfac.

**Timeline:** 0s I / Presence → 3s II / Movement → 6s III / Memory → 10s IV / Accumulation → 15s V / Dissolution → 18s VI / Renewal. Duration 20s.

**Planned review positions:** 0, 4, 8, 12, 16, 20 seconds.
