The Materiality of Motion.
Six ways of seeing.

Each work is an independent time-based composition. Pause, move through the timeline, and notice what the intermediate states reveal. All fonts and artwork assets are local.

所有作品皆可暫停與逐時檢視；減少動態偏好會保留代表性靜態構圖。創作筆記與驗證紀錄隨原始碼提供。
