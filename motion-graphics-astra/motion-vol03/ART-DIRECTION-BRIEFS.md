# The Illusion of Perception — pre-implementation direction

The following briefs define inquiry, visual language, composition, palette, typography, choreography, emotion, memorable moment and technical approach before rendering.

## 13 — Please remain in the picture

**Inquiry / concept:** A transmission tries to hold a sentence together. Its errors turn the space between words into a second message.

Inquiry: can a failed transmission hold a new image? Core: language and electronic memory. Visual language: archival olive-black ink, broad condensed STAY / WITH / ME and a small serif request. Composition: the last word sits far right, leaving a charged central interval. Colour: bone paper, olive-black, one dull gold ghost. Type: Anton for the broadcast; Bodoni for the human voice. Choreography: localized horizontal drift, delayed duplicate, then vertical signal compression; each disturbance has a slow attack and recovery. Emotion: tender unease. Opening key: three clear typographic blocks. Transformation key: a displaced horizontal region crosses the reading. Climax key: WITH becomes a horizon connecting STAY and ME. Memorable moment: a corrupted sentence becomes a landscape. Technique: cached typographic canvas and analytic scanline remapping, with no random flicker.

**Palette:** #ddd9c9 / #292e27 / #a58e49.

**Timeline:** 0s I / Transmission → 2s II / Drift → 5s III / Interference → 8s IV / Another image → 12s V / Restoration. Duration 16s.

**Planned review positions:** 0, 3.2, 6.4, 9.6, 12.8, 16 seconds.

## 14 — An address that cannot exist

**Inquiry / concept:** Three convincing fragments of space refuse to agree on what is in front. The eye keeps building an address that geometry cannot inhabit.

Inquiry: can a form exist only in projection? Core: the eye trusts local joins even when global depth contradicts them. Visual language: mineral-green architectural beams and ivory cut faces, precisely authored in 2D. Composition: an off-axis object floats above a large serif Elsewhere; fine edges are structural, never fake coordinates. Colour: three related values make depth persuasive. Typography: sparse Bodoni and Manrope. Opening key: a Necker-like room with reversible near/far edges. Transformation key: three prism beams form a cyclic over/under relation A>B>C>A. Climax key: three round-ended prongs join only two rectangular supports. Emotion: calm intellectual discomfort. Memorable moment: counting the prongs becomes unreliable. Technique: vector geometry, local occlusion patches and a slow curtain wipe between configurations.

**Palette:** #c9d3ce / #324d47 / #faf0d8.

**Timeline:** 0s I / A plausible room → 2s II / Depth reverses → 5s III / Circular authority → 9s IV / Three become two → 13s V / Reconstruction. Duration 16s.

**Planned review positions:** 0, 3.2, 6.4, 9.6, 12.8, 16 seconds.

## 15 — Two inks, a third voice

**Inquiry / concept:** Two plates meet imperfectly. The image that emerges belongs to neither ink alone.

Inquiry: can the printing structure become the subject? Core: two individual plates create an emergent third voice. Visual language: precise spot-colour printing on warm pale paper. Composition: two cropped planetary forms occupy the upper/right field; a large serif title anchors the lower left. Colour: muted rose and petrol blue multiply into deep aubergine. Typography: editorial Bodoni contrasted with restrained Manrope. Opening key: a soft density disc of round dots. Transformation key: curved density is rebuilt with directional lines. Climax key: diamond lattice overprints the second plate while the forms overlap. Emotion: tactile, rhythmic confidence. Memorable moment: the third colour has no separate ink plate. Technique: analytic density fields, three genuinely different screen geometries, multiply compositing and fixed registration offsets; no raster noise texture.

**Palette:** #f0dfcc / #ad4e60 / #315a75.

**Timeline:** 0s I / Printed order → 3s II / Plate separation → 6s III / Line screen → 9s IV / Overprint → 12s V / Diamond screen → 15s VI / Reprinting. Duration 18s.

**Planned review positions:** 0, 3.6, 7.2, 10.8, 14.4, 18 seconds.

## 16 — Rooms for a small ensemble

**Inquiry / concept:** A small ensemble builds a room one note at a time. Rhythm lays the floor, melody raises the arches, and harmony decides how much air remains.

Inquiry: can musical form become spatial composition without a spectrum display? Core: one written score governs both image and sound. Visual language: a low architectural ensemble of repeated arches, floor tiles and sliding harmonic planes. Composition: receding arches rise on the right, rhythmic floor runs left to right, a small title occupies quiet upper space. Colour: midnight teal, parchment, brass and clay. Typography: understated Bodoni; score notes are meaningful, not dashboard ornament. Opening key: a spare stage with dormant structural outlines. Transformation key: staggered arch entries correspond to melody notes above syncopated floor events. Climax key: two melodic phrases interleave over a sustained chord plane. Emotion: warm precision and anticipation. Memorable moment: a rest opens a room in the middle of the music. Technique: a deterministic 16-second, 120 BPM original score, native Web Audio oscillators and an audio-clock anchored scheduler; default silent, explicitly enabled sound.

**Palette:** #162f36 / #d8ba83 / #e4e4ce / #b57051.

**Timeline:** 0s I / An empty room → 2s II / Foundation → 4s III / Conversation → 8s IV / Counterpoint → 12s V / Cadence → 15s VI / Breath. Duration 16s.

**Planned review positions:** 0, 3.2, 6.4, 9.6, 12.8, 16 seconds.

## 17 — The colour between us

**Inquiry / concept:** The same colour lives two different lives. Its neighbours, its territory and the edge it shares decide which one we see.

Inquiry: is colour a property or a relationship? Core: a constant peach swatch changes perceived temperature through adjacent fields. Visual language: luminous hard-edge painting, no gradient or blur. Composition: unequal russet and straw fields; paired equal-colour windows; a dark mineral base. Typography: one modest ivory caption and deliberately small functional labels. Opening key: equal peach squares on different grounds. Transformation key: field-area dominance reverses while the squares retain their colour. Climax key: the boundaries cut an implied arch from the straw field; the two squares become a single shared band. Emotion: lucid surprise. Memorable moment: two apparently different colours visibly meet without a seam. Technique: exact flat RGB fills, smoothly interpolated edge geometry and a constant swatch value; relationships change, not the swatch.

**Palette:** #a44832 / #d4b969 / #303e39 / #d99b74.

**Timeline:** 0s I / Two neighbours → 3s II / Territory → 6s III / Simultaneous contrast → 9s IV / A boundary becomes a room → 13s V / New balance. Duration 16s.

**Planned review positions:** 0, 3.2, 6.4, 9.6, 12.8, 16 seconds.

## 18 — The guest is the gap

**Inquiry / concept:** Two figures leave a place for someone who never arrives. That absence becomes a vessel, a doorway, and finally the figure itself.

Inquiry: can empty space perform? Core: boundaries shared by two solids compose an absent third subject. Visual language: two precise charcoal profiles on ivory, without texture. Composition: a tall paired silhouette above a small serif sentence; the central gap is the true protagonist. Colour: only bone and charcoal. Typography: a quiet Bodoni line stays clear of the silhouette. Opening key: two abstract side masses with a narrow interval. Transformation key: opposing profile boundaries reveal a vessel in negative space. Climax key: a horizontally moving curtain conceals the vessel neck and reveals a door; a local figure-ground inversion turns the empty vessel into dark matter. Emotion: silent discovery. Memorable moment: the gap acquires weight without another object entering. Technique: paired complementary cubic contours and occlusion masks, with a slow spatial wipe for inversion instead of full-frame flashing.

**Palette:** #ebe5d6 / #282d28.

**Timeline:** 0s I / Presence → 2s II / Separation → 4s III / A vessel appears → 7s IV / The ground performs → 10s V / A door in the silence → 12s VI / Return. Duration 14s.

**Planned review positions:** 0, 2.8, 5.6, 8.4, 11.2, 14 seconds.
