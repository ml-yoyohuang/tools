Gathering

Light

4:12

A year spent collecting the light that falls through one window, at one hour of the afternoon.

For a year I sat at the same table at the same hour and waited for the light to arrive. It came through the north window at 4:12 in the afternoon — a little earlier in winter, a little later in June — and it never came the same way twice.

I began to keep it the way other people keep leaves or stones: a photograph, a note, the length of a shadow on the wall. By December I understood that I was not collecting light at all, only the places where it had briefly agreed to rest.
